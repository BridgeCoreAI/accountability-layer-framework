## What Is ALF?

The Accountability Layer Framework (ALF) is the organizational accountability architecture that operates above the technical enforcement layer of AI governance systems. ALF is derived from the BridgeCore Governance Engineering Methodology™ (BGEM) and published through the Governance Systems Engineering Lab (GSEL).

Where the Execution Governance Model (EGM) enforces governance at runtime, ALF governs the human layer above that enforcement — defining who is accountable, how escalation is structured, what evidence is required, and how post-incident review is governed.

## The Problem ALF Solves

Most AI governance frameworks define technical controls. Fewer define the organizational accountability structures that must operate above those controls.

ALF closes this gap by establishing four formal accountability components that ensure governance decisions are attributed to named humans, escalated through tamper-resistant pathways, supported by structured evidence, and followed by governed post-incident review.

---

## Core ALF Components

ALF v4.0 defines four organizational accountability components:

| Component | What It Establishes |
|---|---|
| Named Authority Registration | Every governance decision is assigned to a named, registered accountable authority. Accountability cannot be anonymous or delegated without record. |
| Escalation Information Package | When governance decisions escalate, they carry a structured evidence package ensuring escalation reviewers have complete context. |
| Pressure-Resistant Accountability | Governance structures that remain accountable under organizational pressure. Authority cannot be informally overridden. |
| Post-Incident Review Protocol | Every governance event triggers a structured post-incident review. Findings are documented and fed back into the governance lifecycle. |

## ALF and EGM — Complementary Architecture

ALF and EGM are complementary engineering components within the BGEM architecture:

```
EGM — Runtime Enforcement
Enforces governance at the moment of AI execution.
Every action passes through the enforcement boundary.
        ↕
ALF — Organizational Accountability
Governs the human layer above that enforcement.
Named authorities, structured escalation, evidence preservation,
and post-incident review.
```

Together EGM and ALF close the gap between technical enforcement and organizational accountability.
