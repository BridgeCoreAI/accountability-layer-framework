## Overview

The Accountability Layer Framework (ALF) is the organizational accountability architecture that operates above the technical enforcement layer of AI governance systems.

ALF is derived from the BridgeCore Governance Engineering Methodology™ (BGEM) and published through the Governance Systems Engineering Lab (GSEL). Where the Execution Governance Model (EGM) enforces governance at runtime, ALF governs the human layer above that enforcement — defining who is accountable, how escalation is structured, what evidence is required, and how post-incident review is governed.

## Architecture

![BridgeCore AI Accountability Layer Framework v4.0](./assets/alf-v4-accountability-layer-diagram.png)

ALF operates as a seven-stage accountability cycle sitting directly on top of the technical enforcement layer (EGM). When EGM surfaces a decision, it moves through named authority registration, a structured escalation information package, and an authority decision made under one of three defined organizational-design requirements — resulting in execution, further escalation, or refusal. Every outcome produces a linked evidence record, triggers post-incident review where accountability failed, and feeds an organizational learning record back into the next cycle.

---

## Core ALF Components

| Component | What It Establishes |
|---|---|
| Named Authority Registration | Every governance decision is assigned to a named, registered accountable authority |
| Escalation Information Package | Structured evidence package carried through every escalation |
| Pressure-Resistant Accountability | Governance structures that remain accountable under organizational pressure |
| Post-Incident Review Protocol | Structured governed review after every governance event |

---

## Framework Control Mapping

ALF accountability structures are mapped to:

- ISO/IEC 42001 — AI management system accountability requirements
- EU AI Act — Human oversight and accountability obligations
- NIST AI RMF — Govern function accountability structures
- NIST SP 800-53 — Personnel and accountability controls
